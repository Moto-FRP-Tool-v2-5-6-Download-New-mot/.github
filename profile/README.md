## 🔒 FRP and Security Information

**FRP is an anti-theft security mechanism.**

Google explains that a protected Android device can require the owner's Google Account or screen lock after an unauthorized factory reset. Removing the Google Account from a device is the normal way to turn off device protection before legitimately transferring the phone to another person.

Motorola likewise recommends backing up data and knowing the associated Google Account credentials before resetting a phone. Motorola says that removing the Google Account and screen lock before giving a device to someone else disables FRP protection.

Use FRP-related functions only for devices you own or are explicitly authorized to service.
